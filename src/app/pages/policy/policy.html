2. Which data we process

When you use Join, the following data is processed:

  • Contact data you enter: name, email address and phone number of the contacts you create, plus the colour automatically assigned to each contact.
  • Task data you enter: title, description, due date, category, subtasks and the contacts you assign to a task.
  • Technical data: when the application is loaded, your browser transmits data such as IP address, browser type, operating system and time of the request to our hosting provider. This data is required to deliver the application.

Join uses no tracking, no analytics and no advertising cookies.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We process the data you enter solely to provide the functions of the application — storing your contacts and tasks and showing them to you again on your next visit. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating the application, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Technical data is processed on the same legal basis for the purpose of secure and stable delivery.

Please note: the data you enter is not protected by a user account. Anyone with access to this application can see, edit and delete all contacts and tasks. Do not enter data of other people without their consent, and do not enter sensitive data.
